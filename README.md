# GDS / GSC-X — Research Publications

**Scientific Publications, Validation Reports and Research Archive**

This repository is the public publication archive of the **GDS Research Institute** and the **GDS / GSC-X research ecosystem** developed by Martin Štěpánů.

It provides a central repository for scientific papers, technical reports, validation reports and supporting research documents covering the development of the Gravitational Dynamical System (GDS), Generative Stability Control (GSC), GSC-X Executive architecture and associated cross-domain research.

---

## Research Ecosystem

The publication portfolio covers several interconnected research programmes.

### GDS — Gravitational Dynamical System

A theoretical and computational framework investigating nonlinear gravitational dynamics, cosmology, gravitational collapse and emergent field behaviour.

### GSC — Generative Stability Control

A family of nonlinear stability-control concepts developed for dynamic systems operating near instability, transition or metastable regimes.

### GSC-X — Executive Architecture

A vendor-independent Executive architecture for predictive monitoring, adaptive control and dynamic system management across heterogeneous engineering and scientific domains.

### Regenerative Metastability

Research into metastable dynamical regimes, recovery behaviour and nonlinear stability mechanisms across complex systems.

## Repository Purpose

This repository serves primarily as a **publication and research-document archive**.

It is intended to provide:

- stable public access to research publications
- English and Czech versions where available

- GDS theoretical publications

## Scientific Scope

The material contained in this repository represents an active independent research programme.

Results should be interpreted within the assumptions, datasets, numerical conditions and experimental limitations documented in each individual publication.

Simulation, replay and digital-twin results are not presented as equivalent to production deployment.

Theoretical results and alternative physical interpretations remain subject to independent reproduction, observational validation and scientific review.
